Feedback during responsible disclosure

One operator told us

They filter the public target used by Is BGP Safe Yet? on purpose: they do not wish to take part in ROV measurements that use Cloudflare-hosted prefixes.

They filtered the public target to opt out, not to improve their score.

Another network

After our disclosure, it began announcing the Is BGP Safe Yet? RPKI-invalid prefix in BGP itself, and kept doing so until the end of our observation.

Public targets can become part of operational routing configurations.

Are we measuring the Internet, or the Internet's response to being measured?
